03
What the problem costs

It is not about the lost hours. It is about three holes through which money leaves

Deals that never close

The contract is signed, the goods shipped, the act completed — and no payment arrives. Nobody notices, because nobody reconciles contracts against the bank statement. A year later it is a receivable that can no longer be collected.

An owner who cannot see

The founder looks at the business once a quarter, through a report full of terms they were never required to understand. By the time the dividends come in lower than expected, it is too late to investigate.

Panic at the moment of risk

A demand arrives from the tax office. The owner has no idea how serious it is, who to turn to or what it should cost. The first outsourcing firm they call can name any number it likes.

In our own database right now there are 388.9 billion UZS of confirmed but unpaid obligations — across 60 of our 80 organisations.

04
Why now

Three catalysts that have coincided

The state made it mandatory

Invoices, waybills, acts and contracts — electronic only, through the e-document system and signed with E-IMZO. Digitisation is not a business decision but a legal requirement: the market was digitised compulsorily and in full.

Open banking — by 01.09.2026

Decree PP-359 mandates standardised access to banking data. Today no bank in the country offers a public banking API. In a month the rules change for everyone at once.

Commerce exploded

E-commerce grew 22-fold in five years; the number of marketplaces went from 36 to 115 in two. Demand for automation was created by the market, not by our advertising.

In places we integrate with what the client already uses — 1C, MoySklad, AmoCRM, Bitrix24, their cash register, their bank. In others we replace it with a module of our own. But always so that the business ends up with one way in and one picture of reality.

An e-invoice in 2 minutes instead of 40 on the portal. By voice, from a phone, digitally signed.

The orchestrator level

07
Defensibility

Three things integrations cannot close

The objection "we will connect our programs with integrations" does not hold: these properties arise only when the data lives in one loop from the outset.

  • The deal as a whole. Contract, payment, power of attorney, e-invoice, act and waybill — one entity with a state and an answer to "whose move is it". An integration passes records, not meaning. We hold 85,875 deals, 73% of them closed.
  • The owner sees the truth. The detector for duplicate payments, structured amounts and turnover without documents works only because it sees money, documents, stock and the till at once. No single program sees that.
  • An AI that knows the business. "Invoice Artel for the same as last time but at the new price" — an assistant plugged into one program out of seven cannot construct that answer.

Any one module can be copied. Copying the combination means building the whole platform.

08
The main bet

The owner talks. The business works

Not voice input and not commands to an assistant. A manager — an AI that knows this company's products and prices, its counterparties and how reliably they pay, its stock, its debts and its obligations.

Why this is possible only here

The voice does not sit on top of the product but inside its core: it invokes the same audited operations the interface buttons use. Every new product feature becomes a voice feature immediately — we do not write it twice.

Where this is going

99% of the operational work is done by the agent. 1% — where a human decision is needed — it brings to the owner. A payment arrives → the invoice is produced, signed, sent and the deal closed. The owner sees a receipt, not a question.

For thirty years, running a business has meant fewer and fewer clicks. We are removing the need to know where to click.

Competition

A market crowded in its parts and empty as a whole

15 e-document operators

Soliqservis, Faktura.uz, Didox, E-docs, L-Factura and ten more. All solve one problem — delivering a document. None deals with what happens around it. We work on top of that layer and do not compete with them.

15+ accounting and trading packages

BILLZ, SmartUp, MoySklad, Bito ERP, Regos, HIPPO and others. Each covers its own patch: the till, the warehouse, a CRM, purchasing.

A solution uniting e-documents + warehouse + CRM + bank + tax office in one loop does not exist on this market. We compete not with a program but with an assembly of seven to nine programs.

Near-term catalysts

How it was built

A platform on an enterprise scale, assembled with AI

A volume of work that normally takes 40–60 engineers and a couple of years was built almost single-handedly, with AI as the primary development tool. That is not a curiosity but a structural advantage in cost and speed.

Engineering discipline

Circuit breakers on nine external APIs, idempotent signing, 108 feature switches, and a nightly scan of secrets, dependencies and images.

Verifiable documentation

A 4,000-line overview in which every figure is reconciled against the code automatically. A new person gets into the product in a day rather than a quarter.

Concentration risk — acknowledged

A key dependency on one person. It is removed by hiring with investment money; the documentation and tests are built so that a handover is possible.
